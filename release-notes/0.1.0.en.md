The first release of Melogold for Linux.

- YouTube Music songs and YouTube videos side by side: search with suggestions, Trends, New, albums, artists, channels and playlists, YouTube links.
- Library: Favorites, your playlists, History by device, All tracks, saved albums and artists, downloads and Save as file.
- Lyrics: synced lyrics with a pill behind the current line, duets and backing vocals, LrcLib search, LRC and TTML import, an editor that syncs by tapping.
- A Melogold server account keeps Favorites, playlists, history, your own titles and lyrics the same on Android, Windows, Apple and Linux within seconds.
- Sleep timer, speed, loudness normalization, MPRIS, Save a copy and import from ViTune and ViMusic.

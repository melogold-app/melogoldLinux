- A new look: Now Playing and the player bar take their colors from the artwork, covers cast shadows, headings are bigger, "Moods & genres" tiles are filled with color and the Library has colored icons. ♡ and "…" on a track show up on hover.
- Insights: Library › "Insights" — how much you listened this week, month, year and all time, your top tracks, artists and albums, when you listened and what you discovered. In December, "Year in review" cards you can save as a picture.
- Sign in with a code: "Sign in with a code" on the sign-in screen, and "Show a code for a new device" in Account › "Add device". No need to type your password on a new computer.
- Links: "Copy link" on a track, album or playlist; your own playlist becomes a link on your Melogold server, your links are in Account. Spotify, Apple Music, Yandex Music, Deezer and Tidal links open in Melogold: paste one into Search.
- Remote: the "Device" button in the player controls the music and volume on another of your devices through the server; "Listen here" takes playback over. Turn it off in Settings › "Control from other devices".
- A device removed from your account no longer shows up in the device filter of History and Insights.
- When YouTube asks to confirm you're not a bot, the player tells you what to do (another VPN server or wait) and doesn't skip through the queue for nothing.
- Fixed: "Add to playlist" for selected tracks and your playlist's menu ("Rename", "Copy link", "Delete") did nothing.
